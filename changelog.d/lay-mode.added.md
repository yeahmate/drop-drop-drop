- Lay: one mark at a time in large type, with the reciprocal a GPS should
  read, the anchor drop offset, live guidance from the phone's GPS and a
  ping to record where each mark was laid.
- Line: square start line bearings, bias and pin move, pin-end boat anchor
  bearings and a tolerance check.
- Wind: a wind log with mean, range, skew from the axis, shift advice and
  the VHF wording for the report.
- The course, reference point, wind log and laid marks are saved on the
  device.
